Research

One interface. Two global challenges.

Making fertiliser chemicals without fossil heat, and storing electricity safely at scale, are both decided in the same few nanometres where an electrode meets an electrolyte. I design that interface.

Green ammonia

Turning N₂ and nitrate into ammonia at room temperature with renewable electricity.

ACS Nano · Small · Adv. Funct. Mater.

Electrochemical urea

Coupling carbon and nitrogen on dual active sites to make fertiliser from CO₂.

J. Mater. Chem. A · Adv. Funct. Mater.

Nitric acid & molecular catalysts

Tuning single metal centres in phthalocyanines to drive selective nitrogen oxidation.

Adv. Funct. Mater. 2024

Batteries & solid electrolytes

Cathodes, solid-state electrolytes and the interfaces that set capacity and lifetime.

Energy & Environ. Sci. 2026
