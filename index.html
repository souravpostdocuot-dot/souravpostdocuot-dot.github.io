Research

One interface. Two global challenges.

Making fertiliser chemicals without fossil heat, and storing electricity safely at scale, are both decided in the same few nanometres where an electrode meets an electrolyte. I design that interface.

Energy conversion: clean chemicals from air, water and waste

Green ammonia from air

Reducing N₂ to ammonia at room temperature with renewable electricity.

ACS Nano · Small · ACS Sustain. Chem. Eng.

Wastewater to ammonia

Turning nitrate pollution into ammonia: cleaner water and fertiliser in one step.

Electrochemical urea

Coupling carbon and nitrogen on dual active sites to make fertiliser from CO₂.

J. Mater. Chem. A · Adv. Funct. Mater.

Nitric acid & molecular catalysts

Tuning single metal centres in phthalocyanines to drive selective nitrogen oxidation.

Adv. Funct. Mater. 2024 · ACS AMI

Energy storage: sodium and lithium batteries

Inside a sodium-ion battery, Na⁺ ions shuttle into Prussian blue cathode crystals. Using first-principles calculations, we showed how the water trapped in those crystals controls sodium access, iron redox and compatibility with both liquid and halide solid-state electrolytes.

  • Energy & Environmental Science, 2026 · Crystal-water regulation governs Prussian blue cathodes in liquid and halide solid-state batteries
